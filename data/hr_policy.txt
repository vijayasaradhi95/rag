# XYZ COMPANY

## EMPLOYEE HR POLICY HANDBOOK

**Demo Document**

---

### 1. LEAVE POLICY

All full-time employees are entitled to **18 days of paid annual leave** per calendar year.

Leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least **5 working days in advance**, except in cases of emergencies.

Unused annual leave may be carried forward to the following year, up to a maximum of **6 days**.

Employees are entitled to **10 paid sick leave days** per calendar year.

A medical certificate may be required for sick leave exceeding **2 consecutive working days**.

---

### 2. WORK FROM HOME POLICY

Employees may work from home for up to **2 days per week**, subject to prior approval from their reporting manager.

Fully remote work arrangements require **written approval from the department head**.

Employees working remotely must remain available during the company's core working hours of **10:00 AM to 4:00 PM**.

Employees are responsible for maintaining a suitable and secure working environment while working from home.

---

### 3. PROBATION PERIOD

All newly hired employees will undergo a **3-month probation period** beginning from their date of joining.

During the probation period, employees are generally not eligible for paid annual leave. Emergency unpaid leave may be granted with manager approval.

Employee performance, conduct, attendance, and role suitability will be reviewed before completion of the probation period.

Based on the review, the company may confirm employment, extend probation, or take other appropriate action in accordance with company policy.

---

### 4. NOTICE PERIOD

Employees who wish to resign from XYZ Company must provide **30 days' written notice**.

Employees who resign during the probation period are required to serve a **15-day notice period**.

The company may waive or reduce the notice period at its discretion, subject to applicable approvals.

Full and final settlement will generally be processed within **45 days from the employee's last working day**, subject to completion of the exit and clearance process.

---

### 5. REIMBURSEMENT POLICY

Employees may claim reimbursement for approved business-related expenses, including **business travel, client meals, transportation, and internet expenses incurred for official work**.

All reimbursement claims must be submitted through the designated expense management system along with valid supporting bills or receipts.

Claims must normally be submitted within **30 days of the date of expense**.

Approved claims will generally be processed within **10 working days** after receiving the required approvals and supporting documentation.

---

### 6. CODE OF CONDUCT

All employees are expected to maintain **professionalism, integrity, and respect** in the workplace.

XYZ Company does not tolerate harassment, discrimination, bullying, retaliation, or other forms of workplace misconduct.

Violations of the Code of Conduct may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment, depending on the nature and severity of the violation.

All employees must complete the company's **annual Code of Conduct training**.

Employees are encouraged to report suspected violations through the appropriate HR or reporting channels.

---

### 7. HOLIDAYS

XYZ Company observes **12 public holidays** each year.

The official holiday calendar will be published by the HR department at the beginning of each calendar year.

Employees required to work on a designated public holiday may be eligible for **compensatory leave**, subject to applicable company rules and manager approval.

Holiday schedules may vary depending on the employee's work location or business requirements.

---

### 8. EXIT POLICY

Employees leaving XYZ Company through resignation or termination must complete the required **exit and clearance process** before their last working day, wherever applicable.

The clearance process may involve the **IT, Finance, Administration, and HR departments**.

Employees must return company-owned assets, including laptops, access cards, documents, and other equipment, before completing the exit process.

Full and final settlement may include pending salary, approved reimbursements, applicable leave encashment, and other eligible payments.

The full and final settlement will generally be processed within **45 days of the employee's last working day**, subject to completion of all required clearance formalities.

---

### 9. EMPLOYEE DATA & CONFIDENTIALITY

Employees must protect confidential company information, customer information, employee information, and other proprietary business data.

Company information must not be shared with unauthorized individuals or external parties without appropriate authorization.

Employees are expected to follow XYZ Company's information security and data protection requirements while using company systems and resources.

Any suspected loss, unauthorized access, or disclosure of confidential information must be reported promptly to the appropriate department.

---

### 10. PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT

Employees will participate in periodic performance reviews based on their role, responsibilities, objectives, and business requirements.

Performance discussions may include goal setting, feedback, development opportunities, and areas for improvement.

Managers are responsible for providing employees with appropriate feedback and guidance throughout the performance cycle.

Performance outcomes may be considered for career development, compensation reviews, promotions, or other employment decisions, subject to company policies.

---

### 11. POLICY COMPLIANCE

All employees are responsible for understanding and complying with the policies described in this handbook.

XYZ Company reserves the right to modify, update, or replace company policies based on business requirements, applicable laws, or organizational changes.

Employees will be notified of significant policy updates through appropriate company communication channels.

Where applicable, local laws and regulations will take precedence over conflicting provisions of this handbook.
